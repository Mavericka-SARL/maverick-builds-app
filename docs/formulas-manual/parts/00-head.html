Formulas Manual

The formula language of calculated metrics: every function, how a formula reads the model, and how its results total
Edition: 2026-10-17, describing the formula engine of release 2026.10.17
Audience: people with the developer role who write calculated metrics, and anyone reviewing a model's formulas
Scope: the formula language and the Metrics screen. The rest of the console is in the Developer Manual. No API calls, no scripts
Verified by: every function and example is asserted by internal/formula/catalogue_test.go; every recipe in chapter 16 was run live, cell by cell, by cmd/verify-formula-catalogue
Source: docs/formulas-manual/parts/*.html, rebuilt into this page and its PDF with build.sh
