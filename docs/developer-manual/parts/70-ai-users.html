AI Developer

Sidebar: Build › AI Developer

A chat assistant that can read the model and, when you approve, build in it: metrics, dimensions and members, grids, dashboards and widgets, revisions. Every write happens in an isolated AI draft revision that you promote or discard, so the live model is never touched by accident.

What it can do

  • Read tools: describe metrics, dimensions, grids and dashboards, validate formulas, answer questions about the model and attached documents.
  • Write tools, always through an approved plan: create, update and delete metrics; create dimensions and add or update members; create grids and attach metrics and dimensions; create dashboards and add widgets; create revisions; generate and apply migrations.
  • Plans are capped at 50 steps; long tasks are continued in a follow-up plan.
Workflows and forms. The assistant builds the whole chain a workflow needs: the business roles its steps are assigned to, the definition with every step type the editor offers, the automation rule that starts it, and the form and form integration that feed it. It runs the same Validate check as the Workflows editor and reports the verdict with every change. Two things stay yours: publishing a workflow (the assistant leaves it as a draft and tells you so) and adding members to a role. The assistant uses the same validation as the screens, so a formula or a workflow it proposes is rejected under the same rules you would be.

Users

Sidebar: Govern › Users (hidden when an admin section already provides it)

The developer's Users screen invites people and gives them business roles, so that the applications you build have users. Deciding which applications and models a person may reach, and the platform roles above business level, stay with tenant and platform administrators.

ControlWhat it does
Invite userEmail, First name, Last name, Initial Role, Workspace. The person receives an invitation to set up their account; the email is the sign-in identity and cannot be changed later.
Role controlsA developer can grant and revoke business_admin and business_user only. Developer, tenant admin and platform admin roles are granted by administrators.
Workspace rolesPlace a person into a workspace with a business role. The Resource access section (which applications and models a person may open) is shown to administrators only.
Delete userConfirm Delete user?: removes the person and revokes all their access. You cannot delete your own account.

Where the rest of access is configured

Three things decide what a business user sees and may change. All three live in the Business Admin console (sidebar group Admin), which a developer sees only when they also hold the business_admin role or ask a business admin:

  • Roles. Named business roles ("Budget Approvers", "Regional Managers") with members. Workflow tasks and approvals are assigned to these roles or to the platform roles.
  • Dashboard access. Per role, tick the dashboards its members may open (Save dashboard access). A dashboard on no role is invisible to business users.
  • Access rules. Per user, an Access level of write, read or hidden for individual dimension members and metrics (Save rules). Hidden members disappear from grids, charts and selectors; read makes cells read-only; rules on a parent member cascade to its descendants.
